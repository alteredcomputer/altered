# Framework: The Offer (WHAT / WHO / WHY)

Inherits from: S2, S3, S7/S7a, S14, S21–S26, S32, S33.

## WHY (the root problem)

High-detail builders lose their best thinking. Ideas, decisions, and hard-won WHYs scatter across notes, chats, and memory — so under pressure they re-derive conclusions, pivot away from settled direction, and repeat anti-patterns. The project never ships; the cycle repeats. (This is the operator's lived pain, 5x-converged; the operator is the case study.)

## WHAT

**ALTERED Koa** — an always-on iMessage agent with infinite memory of everything you've told it, that reaches out on its own schedule to keep you locked on the goal. Layer 1 of the ALTERED platform.

**Tagline (locked, S21):** *Never lose your best thinking again.*

**Program (locked, S23):** **ALTERED: Layer 1** — $221 USD one-time · 6 months · $50 usage balance (Cursor-style) · 30-min 1-1 onboarding call · Discord · first-party feature prioritization · being-built tools (structured thinking / thought editor, system builder) as roadmap framing. 30-day no-questions refund with save-flow (S24). No trial — direct sale in iMessage (S32).

## WHO

Detail-obsessed technical founders on X, building arbitrary/novel concepts, blocked from finishing by re-scoping and shifting priorities (S3). Expansion post-revenue: builder/creator/founder/entrepreneur generalization. **Copy discipline:** public copy says "founders"/"technical founders" — "creators" is season two; do not let it drift into cohort-1 assets.

### Word bank (with rationale)

- **"pressure pivots"** — operator coinage, keep everywhere. Distinguishes the failure mode (pivoting under pressure) from smart strategic pivots, which this demographic celebrates. "Pivots" alone is ambiguous-positive.
- **"redundant thinking"** — broader than "redundant conclusions"; alt for long-form: "re-deriving conclusions" (vivid for technical readers).
- **"anti-patterns"** — demographic-native, carries quality-control connotation. Long-form only; two pains max in short copy (three reads as a list).
- **"eliminates" / "kills" / "prevents"** — eliminates for master copy (strong + credible), kills for social (punch), prevents is the soft fallback.
- **"detail-obsessed"** over "high-detail" — idiomatic, and carries the obsession thread from "Knowledge Systems for the Obsessed."
- **"resurfaces"** — the mechanism verb. Pair with a possessive ("your best thinking", "what you'd otherwise lose") to echo the tagline; never bare "resurfacing conclusions."
- **"iMessage agent"** — names the medium; carries the ergonomics claim natively. Never "ergonomic agent" (insider word, cold traffic feels nothing).
- **"locked on the goal (until it ships)"** — the close. Outcome = progress-to-completion (S25); "until it ships" lands it for SaaS builders.
- **"always-on"** — signals no-downtime + autonomy (self-scheduled reach-outs); keep in master + X bio, droppable at IG char pressure.

## Fall-back / fall-forward mechanism

When conversion breaks (or wins), adjust the **shallowest layer first** — never re-open a deeper layer without an evidence chain through the layers above it:

> messaging/copy → proof assets → funnel mechanics → price → offer stack → demographic (S3) → root problem (S2)

- **Fall-back:** a failing metric at layer N is first treated as a layer-1..N-1 problem. Example: no DM replies ≠ "wrong demographic" — it's script/proof first, at n≥30 sends before judgment.
- **Fall-forward:** when a layer wins, deepen commitment along the same chain — scale outreach volume, then raise price (cohort 2: $442+), then expand demographic per the S3 path. Never expand two layers at once.
- Every adjustment is dated + recorded with its trigger metric in `.context/marketing-generated/` so the offer cannot silently twist (the operator's stated fear: advertising non-core components because brand/offer weren't locked end-to-end).
